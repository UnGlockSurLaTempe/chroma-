# ADC laning fundamentals

Last-hitting is your first job. Dropping below ~70 CS at 10 minutes in a ranged
matchup usually means you are either getting zoned, mispositioning for minions,
or recalling at bad times. Track which it is: if you are zoned, the problem is
your support's lane presence and your trade pattern, not your mechanics.

Trading pattern vs Caitlyn: she out-ranges most ADCs at 650. Do not walk up to
last-hit when her trap is set or her headshot is up. Use minion waves as cover
and look to all-in only when she has used her net (E) to disengage.

When you die in lane before 14 minutes, the cost is compounding: you lose CS,
you lose tower plates, and you give the enemy bot lane tempo to roam or take
dragon. One early death is recoverable; three is a lost lane and you must shift
to a safe farming + scaling plan rather than forcing trades.

# Recovering a losing lane

If you are behind in gold and CS, stop contesting the wave at the enemy tower.
Freeze or slow-push from your side, ward your jungle entrances, and farm safely
until your first two completed items come online. A behind ADC who hits three
items alive is still a win condition; a behind ADC who keeps dying is not.
