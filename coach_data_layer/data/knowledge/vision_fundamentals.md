# Vision prevents deaths

Most early bot-lane deaths are gank deaths, and most gank deaths happen with no
ward covering the river or tri-brush entrance. If you are dying repeatedly in the
same map zone, the fix is almost never "play better mechanically" — it is a
warding and wave-state problem.

Place a ward before you push past the river line. As the ADC you do not have to
ward alone — coordinate with your support so one trinket covers river and one
covers tri-brush. When both wards expire, treat the lane as dark and stop
extending.

# Reading the deaths on the timeline

When reviewing a game, plot where the deaths happened and at what minute. A
cluster of deaths in BOT_RIVER before 14:00 is a clear signal: the enemy jungler
pathed bottom, and you had no information. The corrective drill is to ward on a
timer and to respect the enemy jungler's position after they show bot once.
